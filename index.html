Samples the paths of an SVG, computes Fourier coefficients for each, and animates the reconstruction.

Each contour gets its own Fourier series. Fourier series are periodic, so open strokes can gain a joining segment. Basic SVG shapes are supported; complex SVG features such as clipping, masks, filters, and external CSS may not reproduce exactly. More harmonics preserve finer detail but cannot fix missing or unsuitable source geometry.
