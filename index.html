Fourier Curve Animator

Enter a complex Fourier series or parametric equations, then animate the traced curve.

Use t as the parameter. For complex series, use z(t) = Σ cₙ e^(int). Enter the real and imaginary parts as expressions using the syntax below.

Allowed: t, numbers, pi, e, + - * / ^, parentheses, and functions sin cos tan asin acos atan sqrt abs exp log.
Examples: cos(t)+0.5*cos(4*t), sin(2*t), t/2.
Use explicit multiplication: 2*t, not 2t. This evaluates expressions locally in your browser; no server needed.
